# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Restaurant guests in Barcelona who want to discover the menu, understand the restaurant's cooking, find practical visit information, and book a table.

## Product Purpose

Casa Güell's website introduces the restaurant and helps guests explore its food and market-led cooking, reserve a table, find the restaurant, and subscribe to updates about the daily product.

## Positioning

Casa Güell serves Catalan cooking rooted in market produce and slow preparation. The current site states thirty years of experience and daily market buying; the user confirmed the current content and claims may be retained.

## Operating Context

Guests may browse in Catalan, Spanish, or English and use the website on desktop or mobile before visiting the restaurant in Sant Martí, Barcelona.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Explore food, beverages, and spirits, with category filters, dish search, prices, and allergen information.
- Open a table reservation form.
- Find the address, opening hours, phone number, social accounts, and map directions.
- Subscribe to the daily product newsletter.
- Switch between light, dark, and system themes.
- The menu's most recent addition is the "Mar y montaña" dish (butifarra de Can Rovira with sepionets); menu data lives in `src/data/casaGuell.json`.
- Preserve these existing capabilities and the confirmed business content during the requested design improvement.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the Casa Güell name and existing color palette.
- Preserve the current Catalan restaurant identity and its product-focused voice.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing site copy and localization in `src/i18n/locales/ca.ts`, `src/i18n/locales/es.ts`, and `src/i18n/locales/en.ts`; current business claims were confirmed by the user for retention.
- Existing restaurant and food photography under `public/assets/img/`.
- Working menu, booking, newsletter, location, language, and theme interfaces in the source tree.

## Product Principles

- Make the food and market-led cooking easy to discover.
- Help guests move quickly from browsing to booking or finding the restaurant.
- Keep essential information usable across languages and screen sizes.
- Preserve the restaurant's confirmed content and existing identity.
